Roles in consulting

Consulting offers a variety of exciting career paths, with different roles contributing to successful project delivery and organisational change. In this session, you will:

  • Gain an introduction to three key consulting roles: Project Manager, Business Analyst and Change Manager.
  • Understand the responsibilities, skills and approaches used within each role.
  • Explore how consultants deliver projects, solve business problems and support change.
  • Learn about real consulting scenarios and practical tools used by consulting teams.
  • Discover how these skills can be applied within student consulting projects and future consulting careers.

Improving PowerPoint skills

Creating effective presentations is a core consulting skill, helping you communicate insights, influence decisions and build trust with your audience. In this interactive session, you will:

  • Learn how to structure presentations using visual storytelling techniques that make your message clear and memorable.
  • Explore practical approaches for creating impactful slide decks, including audience-focused design and the 4S framework for building presentations efficiently.
  • Develop techniques for presenting data clearly, simplifying complex information and creating visuals that support your key message.
  • Discover tips and tricks to improve slide design, including alignment, formatting, colour usage and attention to detail.
  • Apply your learning through practical activities and real-world examples, helping you create more professional and engaging presentations.

Yes, every session in this series is completely free to attend.

Anyone considering a career in consulting; graduates, students or anyone else who is interested.
